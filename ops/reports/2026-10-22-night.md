## Correction to #33 and the morning report
Both said a phone user "could not tap MTDI". That was wrong in two ways:
- **Phones show the desktop layout by default** (`html.phone-desktop`, held to 1180 px on the long edge). The narrow phone layout is an opt-in in Settings (`i3-phone-layout = mobile`). #33 fixes that opt-in layout and narrow desktop windows. It does not change what a phone shows by default.
- **The original bug was visual**: the nav overlapped the page, and the Back button sat on top of HOME and PAGE 1. The tap-blocking I reported came from an intermediate state of my own fix (bar moved to the bottom, dock not yet lifted). On the code before #33, the tap works.
The fix itself is still right; my description of it overstated the impact.
