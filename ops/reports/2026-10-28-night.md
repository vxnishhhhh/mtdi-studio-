### Features
* **Page 3 says what it is.** Its sections are THE CHANNELS, THE EVIDENCE, THE BASELINES, LISTEN, RECORD, EXPORT and D4 ANALYSIS, and each has a line saying what it holds. The overview defines MTDI and I³.
* **The I³ panels say what each row is.** These are the panels the runtime adds to Page 3, Settings and the MTDI pillars: channel cards, direction, what the channels say, calibration, listen, events, telemetry, camera and export. They name the project's channels in their pickers and rows.
  * Calibration explains the two-point method. Its unit field lists the units the engine accepts (uT, V/m, mV RMS relative or dB) and defaults to uT, which is valid for channel 1. Before, it defaulted to the placeholder "unit".
  * Listen says what is heard. Channel 2 plays its measured signal, which tops out near 430 Hz. Channel 1 is a sonification, a 220 Hz tone that follows the change, as studio/audition.py describes.
  * Export says what the session downloads hold: each channel's recent changes from its baseline, about the last 30 seconds (the engine's history), plus calibrations and events in the JSON. My first draft said "every reading"; I checked the route and corrected it before committing.
* **Header tools and the NVTRA orb:**
  * The camera, mode and voice buttons have names and shortcuts.
  * The orb's pills name the project's channels.
  * The input says "Ask NVTRA".
  * The engine picker reads "Loading engines" until the page fills it.
* **The startup intro** lists the three channels, MTDI, I³ and NVTRA where it had "ITEM 1" to "ITEM 6", and credits the project's author.

### Weaknesses
* **The calibration unit field is free text with one default.** Picking channel 2 and keeping uT is refused by the engine, and the refusal is only reported after you press Apply. A unit list that follows the chosen channel would be better.
* **Some panel text is still the studio's own instrument, not the project's.** The listen panel's 430 Hz and 220 Hz lines describe the studio's own probes. For a project feed, these sources may never start. That is honest for the studio and irrelevant for other projects.
* **`cards_track` and `PILLAR_CARDS` in ui.py still hold placeholders.** Nothing in the studio calls them. I left them alone rather than invent words for dead code.
