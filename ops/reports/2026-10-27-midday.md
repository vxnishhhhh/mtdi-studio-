## Features
* **The D4 engine cards say what they show (#71).** Every D4 card on every page now has real text instead of placeholders: titles, notes, buttons, input hints, readout rows and empty states. Channel names come from your project. Buttons say what they do (FIT versus FIT AND APPLY), and CLEAR and DELETE have a tooltip saying what they remove.
* **The browser tour behaves like a visitor (#70).** It scrolls through the startup intro before using the nav, and gives each page up to 420 s in this GPU-less container.

## Bugs found and fixed
* **D4 read a project's channel 3 sweep as a radio sweep.** It showed MHz, dBm and radio band notes, and did decibel arithmetic on whatever unit the levels were in. Now:
  * a fed sweep keeps its own x unit and its decimals
  * it is analysed only when its levels are in dB
  * its peak is no longer turned into a GHz feature for the classifier
* **The fingerprint compared unlike floors.** It subtracted a radio reference's floor from a fed one, and treated a missing floor as 0. A floor change is now stated only between two sweeps of the same kind and level unit.
* **Channel 1 readouts were labelled with the project's word for the unit, not the data's unit.** They now follow the source: a feed's own unit, or µT for the studio's magnetometer. Each calibration fit keeps the unit it was made in.
* **The exposure note gave the ICNIRP 1998 level as "100 unit".** It was 100 µT.

## Ideas
